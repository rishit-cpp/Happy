A few lines

The candles glow like stars tonight,
a quiet flame for all you give,
your love has been my guiding light,
the gentlest way I've learned to live.

Each year unwraps another year
of laughter kept behind your eyes,
you turn the ordinary clear,
and turn the cloudy into skies.

So here's to you on this bright day,
the heart that taught my heart to beat —
may every wish come out your way,
and every year be just as sweet.
